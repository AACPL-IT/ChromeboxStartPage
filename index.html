Why we’re switching to Chromeboxes

01

Your safety comes first

Our top priorities are keeping your personal information safe and keeping computers available for everyone who needs one. Every choice we make about our public computers starts there.

02

Security problems are growing fast

Security researchers are finding flaws in mainstream operating systems at an alarming rate. Each flaw has to be fixed with an update, and computers waiting on updates can be at risk or out of service. These systems are far more complex than our customers need, and every extra part is another place for something to go wrong.

03

Built for how you use our computers

Our own data shows that a large majority of customers use our computers to browse the internet. Chromeboxes are designed around the web browser, so they do that job well while leaving out much of what makes other operating systems harder to protect.
